    🤹 Juggle

    🛞 Unicycle Juggler

    1. 1
      Step back so your whole body is visible, head to toes. Prop the device up so it can see you.
    2. 2
      Lift one foot off the floor to keep your balance on the unicycle.
    3. 3
      Keep juggling! Catch the balls and flick them up. Balanced time makes your score multiplier grow.
    4. 4
      Both feet down too long, or swaying too far, and you wobble and fall.

    🤸 Jumping Jacks

    1. 1
      Step back so your whole body is visible, head to toes. Prop the device up so it can see you.
    2. 2
      Jump arms up & feet apart, then arms down & feet together. That’s one jack!
    3. 3
      Do as many as you can in 60 seconds. A 3-2-1 countdown starts when we see you.

    ⚽ Soccer

    1. 1
      Put the device on the floor, leaning back against something, with the camera facing up toward you.
    2. 2
      Stand about 6–8 feet (2 m) back so the camera sees your legs and feet. Your whole body is even better!
    3. 3
      Kicker: swing a foot fast like you’re kicking the ball. Faster = harder shot. Swing a bit left or right to aim. 5 shots!
    4. 4
      Goalie: block shots with your hands, feet and body. Lean or lunge to dive!
